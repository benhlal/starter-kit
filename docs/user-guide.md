# User Guide

## Getting Started

### Welcome to AR Coin Hunt!

AR Coin Hunt is an exciting augmented reality game that combines GPS positioning with AR technology to create immersive coin hunting experiences. Whether you're exploring your neighborhood or participating in organized events, this guide will help you make the most of your coin hunting adventures.

### First Time Setup

1. **Download and Install**

   - Download the app from the App Store or Google Play Store
   - Grant all requested permissions (location, camera, etc.)
   - Create your account or sign in

2. **Profile Setup**

   - Add a profile picture and username
   - Set your preferences (theme, notifications)
   - Review the tutorial if available

3. **Permissions**
   - **Location**: Required for GPS positioning and coin hunting
   - **Camera**: Needed for AR experiences
   - **Storage**: For saving photos and app data

## Participating in Events

### Finding Events

1. **Browse Events**

   - Scroll through the Events screen
   - Use filters to find specific types of events
   - Check event details for requirements and rewards

2. **Event Types**

   - **Treasure Hunts**: Traditional coin collecting
   - **AR Experiences**: Immersive augmented reality
   - **Community Events**: Social gatherings
   - **Challenges**: Competitive timed events

3. **Event Status**
   - **Upcoming**: Events you can join in advance
   - **Ongoing**: Currently active events
   - **Completed**: Finished events (view results only)

### Joining Events

1. **Select Event**

   - Tap on an event card to view details
   - Read the description, requirements, and rules
   - Check the location and timing

2. **Join Process**

   - Tap "Join Event" button
   - Review the participation fee (if any)
   - Confirm your participation
   - Note: Ongoing events may have late fees

3. **Participation Fee**
   - **Upcoming Events**: Standard fee (usually 5 coins)
   - **Ongoing Events**: Standard fee + late penalty (10 extra coins)
   - **Free Events**: No coins required

### During Events

#### Coin Hunting Basics

1. **Navigate to Location**

   - Use the map to find the event area
   - Follow GPS directions to the starting point
   - Look for the AR indicator when nearby

2. **AR Coin Collection**

   - Open the AR camera view
   - Look for virtual coins in your surroundings
   - Tap coins to collect them
   - Watch for collection animations and sounds

3. **GPS and Accuracy**
   - Ensure good GPS signal (outdoors preferred)
   - Check the accuracy indicator in the app
   - Move around if GPS is inaccurate

#### Tips for Success

- **Stay Outdoors**: Better GPS accuracy and AR performance
- **Good Lighting**: AR works best in well-lit environments
- **Stable Movement**: Sudden movements can affect AR tracking
- **Battery Management**: AR and GPS drain battery quickly
- **Network Connection**: Some features require internet

### Leaving Events

1. **Early Departure**

   - Tap "Leave Event" in the event details
   - Review refund policy based on timing
   - Confirm leaving (may lose participation fee)

2. **Refund Policy**
   - **Before Start**: Full refund if >5 hours remaining
   - **Before Start**: Partial refund if <5 hours (penalty applies)
   - **After Start**: No refund unless you've collected coins
   - **With Coins**: Full refund + keep collected coins

## AR Features

### AR Camera View

#### Interface Elements

- **Camera Feed**: Live camera view with AR overlays
- **Coin Indicators**: Virtual coins in your surroundings
- **Distance Display**: How far you are from coins
- **Compass**: Directional guidance to coins
- **Collection Button**: Manual collection when close enough

#### AR Best Practices

1. **Device Positioning**

   - Hold device steady and at arm's length
   - Point camera at areas where coins might be
   - Move slowly to scan surroundings

2. **Coin Recognition**

   - Look for glowing or animated coin objects
   - Coins may appear at different heights and angles
   - Some coins require specific viewing angles

3. **Troubleshooting AR**
   - Ensure camera permission is granted
   - Check that device supports AR (most modern phones do)
   - Try restarting the AR session if issues occur

### GPS Integration

#### Understanding GPS

- **Accuracy Indicator**: Shows current GPS precision
- **Signal Strength**: Green (good), Yellow (fair), Red (poor)
- **Location Updates**: Real-time position tracking

#### Improving GPS Performance

- **Outdoor Location**: GPS works best outdoors with clear sky
- **Avoid Interference**: Stay away from tall buildings or dense trees
- **Wait for Accuracy**: Allow time for GPS to get accurate reading
- **High Accuracy Mode**: Enable in device location settings

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't Find Coins

- Check GPS accuracy and signal strength
- Ensure you're in the correct location
- Try restarting the AR camera
- Verify event is still active

#### App Running Slowly

- Close other apps to free up memory
- Restart the app
- Check for app updates
- Clear app cache if available

#### GPS Not Accurate

- Move to an open outdoor area
- Wait for GPS to stabilize
- Check device location settings
- Restart location services

#### AR Not Working

- Grant camera permissions
- Ensure device supports AR
- Try different lighting conditions
- Restart the AR session

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: How do I start playing?**
A: Create an account, grant permissions, and browse available events to join.

**Q: Do I need special equipment?**
A: Just a smartphone with camera and GPS. Most modern phones work great.

**Q: Is the app free to use?**
A: Basic features are free. Some premium features may require coins or purchases.

### Events and Participation

**Q: How do I join events?**
A: Browse events, tap to view details, and click "Join Event" if available.

**Q: What are participation fees for?**
A: Fees help manage event capacity and provide rewards for organizers.

**Q: Can I leave events early?**
A: Yes, but refund policies apply based on timing and participation.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why isn't AR working?**
A: Ensure camera permissions and check device AR compatibility.

**Q: How accurate is the GPS?**
A: Accuracy varies by location and device, typically within 5-10 meters.

**Q: Can I play offline?**
A: Limited offline functionality available, but most features require internet.

### Account and Coins

**Q: How do I earn coins?**
A: Join events, collect virtual coins, complete achievements, and participate regularly.

**Q: What can I spend coins on?**
A: Event fees, profile upgrades, special items, and supporting organizers.

**Q: Are coins real money?**
A: No, coins are in-app currency only, not convertible to real money.

Remember, AR Coin Hunt is about having fun while exploring and connecting with others. Enjoy your coin hunting adventures!
